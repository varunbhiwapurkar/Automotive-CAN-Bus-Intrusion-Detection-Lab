# ISO/SAE 21434 and UNECE R155 Mapping

| Lab Capability | ISO/SAE 21434 Alignment | UNECE R155 Alignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAN attack simulation (spoof/replay/DoS) | Clause 15: Cybersecurity validation and verification activities | Annex 5: Threat and risk management evidence |
| IDS rule-based monitoring | Clause 9/10: Concept and system-level cybersecurity analysis | Annex 5: Detection and monitoring controls |
| Logging and evidence retention (`logs/`, `screenshots/`) | Clause 7: Cybersecurity management and work products | CSMS auditability and incident evidence |
| Continuous improvement roadmap (dashboard + hardened ECU auth) | Clause 6: Organizational cybersecurity governance | Ongoing CSMS maintenance and updates |

## Practical Use in This Lab

- Use attack scripts to demonstrate plausible threat scenarios.
- Use IDS alerts and Wireshark traces as cybersecurity validation artifacts.
- Preserve logs/screenshots for internal reviews and compliance demonstrations.
